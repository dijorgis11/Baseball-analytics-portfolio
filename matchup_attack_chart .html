Conclusion

This is one repeatable data pipeline, not a one-off chart. The same script and shrinkage logic that built these eleven Braves hitters and three time windows extends to any roster, any cutoff, and any additional window just by re-running it. That's what makes it useful beyond a demo: a team could point this at next week's opponent, pull their active roster instead of the Braves', and have a real, league-relative attack chart for every hitter they're about to face, without changing a line of the underlying analysis, only which players and dates go in.
