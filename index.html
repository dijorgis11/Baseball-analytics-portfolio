Pitcher Fatigue Risk Index

Can start-by-start changes flag injury risk before it becomes a medical event?

The index scores every start against the pitcher's own recent baseline in fastball velocity, spin rate, release point, and workload, then flags any start above his personal 85th-percentile risk threshold. I chose a pitcher-specific baseline over a classifier because injuries are rare, every pitcher has a different normal, and coaches need to see exactly which signal moved.

What I found

Tested on Spencer Strider's 2024 UCL injury, both of his final starts crossed the threshold, driven by velocity and spin decline rather than release drift or workload. Several 2023 starts also crossed it with no injury attached, so with one case this is a proof of concept, not proof of prediction.
